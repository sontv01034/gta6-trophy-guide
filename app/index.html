Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

About 200 hours in total (Insider Gaming, via GTA Six Insider), with the story around 50 hours. The list is long, not hard: there are no difficulty trophies, no mission medals and no online trophies. Cheats switch off trophies and saving (GTABase, TechWiser), so leave cheats off for the whole run. The game has no mission replay and no New Game Plus (Output Lag) — keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
